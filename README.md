About the Project
This project is a Sales Data Analysis case study based on a simulated retail dataset.
The dataset contains daily trading information for one product sold at a large retail store. The aim of the analysis is to use the available sales data to calculate important sales and profitability metrics and then use those metrics to understand the performance of the product.
The case study provides four main variables: Date, Sales, Cost of Sales, and Quantity Sold.
________________________________________
Case Study Objective
The main objective of this project is to analyse the sales data and develop useful metrics and insights that can help explain the product’s performance.
The case study requires an analysis of:
•	Daily selling price per unit
•	Average unit selling price
•	Daily gross profit percentage
•	Daily gross profit percentage per unit
•	Performance during promotional periods
•	Price Elasticity of Demand during selected promotions
•	Additional trends, KPIs, visuals and insights that can be found in the data
________________________________________
Dataset
The dataset represents the daily trading activity of a single retail product.
Column	Description
Date	The day on which the sales occurred
Sales	Total Rand value of sales
Cost of Sales	Total Rand value of the cost of sales
Quantity Sold	Total number of units sold
The data is simulated and was provided specifically for this case study.

Analysis
1. Daily Sales Price per Unit
The daily sales price per unit shows the average amount received for each unit sold on a particular day.
Formula
Daily Sales Price per Unit = Sales / Quantity Sold
This metric is useful for identifying changes in the product’s selling price over time.
________________________________________
2. Average Unit Sales Price
The overall average unit sales price is calculated using total sales and total quantity sold.
Formula
Average Unit Sales Price = Total Sales / Total Quantity Sold
This provides an overall view of the price at which the product was sold during the period covered by the dataset.
________________________________________
3. Daily Gross Profit
Gross profit is calculated by subtracting the cost of sales from sales.
Formula
Gross Profit = Sales - Cost of Sales
The daily gross profit percentage is then calculated as:
Gross Profit % = (Sales - Cost of Sales) / Sales × 100
This helps show how profitable the product was on each day.
________________________________________
4. Daily Gross Profit per Unit
Gross profit per unit looks at the profit generated from each unit sold.
Formula
Gross Profit per Unit =
(Sales - Cost of Sales) / Quantity Sold
Looking at profit per unit alongside the selling price helps provide a better understanding of the product’s profitability.
________________________________________
5. Promotional Period Analysis
Three periods were selected from the dataset where the product was considered to be on promotion or special.
For each period, the analysis compares the product’s price and quantity sold to understand how demand changed when the price changed.
Price Elasticity of Demand
Price Elasticity of Demand measures the relationship between a change in price and a change in quantity demanded.
PED = % Change in Quantity Demanded
      --------------------------------
          % Change in Price
The calculated elasticity values are used to assess whether demand was responsive to the promotional price.
The three promotional periods are analysed separately before comparing their results.
Promotional Performance
The promotional analysis also considers whether the increase or decrease in quantity sold was enough to make the promotion worthwhile.
The comparison looks at factors such as:
•	Selling price
•	Quantity sold
•	Sales revenue
•	Gross profit
•	Gross profit per unit
•	Price elasticity
